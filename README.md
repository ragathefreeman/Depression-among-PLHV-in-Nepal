Depressive symptoms and associated factors among people living with HIV receiving antiretroviral therapy in Pokhara, Nepal: 
a cross-sectional study 

- Raghav Khanal (Green Tara Nepal), Amita K.C (Hope International College), Runa Tandukar and Sharada P Wasti

This is a cross-sectional study done in 256 PLHV in Kaski District Nepal in 2019. Nepal Version of BDI was used to assessed depressive symptoms in participant, the study passed the ethical approval from NHRC. We believe in open and reproduciable science therefore we share our study findings, data and codes. Please follow the instruction below for more info:

### manuscript_1.docx is the complete report of this study.
### data2orginal.rds is the data.
### MKDW.Rmd and MKDW.html are the markdown, use R studio to run the code.
### Other files are the output including descriptive summary, test of association and regression table.
